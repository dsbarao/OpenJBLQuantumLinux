# EVT-001 — first passive input sequence

Date: 2026-09-16
Transport: `/dev/hidraw0`, opened read-only
Device: JBL Quantum 810 dongle, `0ecb:2069`
Physical action: headset switched off, then switched on

## Observed sequence

```text
1789599968.803  len=2  09 00
1789599973.843  len=2  09 01
1789599973.844  len=2  02 01
1789599973.846  len=2  06 00
1789599973.847  len=2  10 00
1789599973.848  len=2  07 00
1789599973.849  len=2  03 00
1789599973.906  len=2  08 5a
1789599974.026  len=2  08 5a
1789599974.100  len=2  08 5a
```

## Observations

- Every packet is two bytes: one Report ID plus one payload byte, matching the
  kernel-exported HID report descriptor.
- `09 00` was observed after the headset was switched off.
- `09 01` was observed when the headset was switched on about five seconds
  later.
- Reports `0x02`, `0x06`, `0x10`, `0x07`, and `0x03` followed in rapid order.
- Report `0x08` repeated payload `0x5a` three times over roughly 250 ms.
- The reports following `09 01` form a likely startup/reconnection state burst.

## Hypotheses — unconfirmed

- Report `0x09` likely describes a binary connection/power-presence state:
  payload `0x00` for off/disconnected and `0x01` for on/connected. Separate
  off-only and on-only repetitions are required to distinguish power from
  wireless connection state.
- Decimal `0x5a` is 90, so Report `0x08` may carry a percentage-like value
  such as battery level. Repetition and controlled comparisons are required.

Later isolated repetitions in EVT-002 and EVT-003 confirmed the `0x09`
off/disconnected and on/connected state association. See `EVT-002-003.md`.
