# EVT-009 — Game/Chat balance dial

Date: 2026-09-16
Transport: hidraw, opened read-only
Action: dial moved toward Game, then toward Chat

## Observed reports

```text
toward Game: 10 0c, 10 0e, 10 10
toward Chat: 10 0e, 10 08, 10 02, 10 00
```

The initial `06 00` was the already-muted microphone state and is unrelated to
the dial sequence.

## Confirmed mapping

Report `0x10` carries an absolute Game/Chat balance position:

| Payload | Position |
|---:|---|
| `0x00` | Chat extreme |
| `0x08` | Center |
| `0x10` | Game extreme |

EVT-010 later observed odd intermediate values and disproved the initial
even-only hypothesis. The confirmed numeric range remains 0–16.

ALSA mixer snapshots before and after were byte-for-byte identical (`cmp` exit
0) with SHA-256
`afcdc2b576bcc94c4af2ef19b413c741ab634222d48330149b329afa7f4f0b44`.
The balance is therefore reported through HID but does not directly rewrite
the exposed ALSA mixer controls.
